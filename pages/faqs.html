Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about living at Redwood Tree Apartments.

Where is Redwood Tree Apartments located?

Redwood Tree Apartments is located at 607 E 8th Street, Davis, California 95616, near UC Davis, downtown Davis, bike routes, and public transportation.

What floor plans are available?

The community offers a one-bedroom, one-bath layout of approximately 660 square feet and a two-bedroom, one-bath layout of approximately 820 square feet. Pricing and availability may vary.

Is Redwood Tree Apartments pet-friendly?

Yes. Contact the leasing team for current pet policies, deposits, fees, and any applicable restrictions.

Does the community have a pool?

Yes. Redwood Tree Apartments has a community swimming pool for residents. Pool access is subject to community rules and policies.

Is there a place to study on-site?

Yes. Residents have access to an on-site study room with comfortable seating, a complimentary self-serve coffee bar, and free printing.

Are furnished apartments available?

Furnished apartments may be available upon request for an additional monthly fee. Confirm current options and pricing with the leasing team.

Is laundry available?

The community offers on-site laundry facilities. Contact the leasing team for current facility details and access information.

How can I tour Redwood Tree Apartments?

Request a tour through the leasing team or schedule a self-guided tour at redwoodtreeapartments.mysmarttour.com.

How do I check availability or apply?

Use the “Check Availability” button to review current apartment availability, rates, deposits, and fees. Confirm all details before submitting an application.

Who can apply?

Redwood Tree Apartments welcomes applications from qualified applicants, including students and non-students. Applications are evaluated under the community's rental criteria and applicable fair housing laws.
