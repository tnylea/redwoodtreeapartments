Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about living at Redwood Tree Apartments.

Where is Redwood Tree Apartments located?

Redwood Tree Apartments is located at 607 E 8th Street, Davis, California 95616, near UC Davis, downtown Davis, bike routes, and public transportation.

What floor plans are available?

The community offers a one-bedroom, one-bath layout of approximately 660 square feet and a two-bedroom, one-bath layout of approximately 820 square feet. Pricing and availability may vary.

Is Redwood Tree Apartments pet-friendly?

Yes. Contact the leasing team for current pet policies, deposits, fees, and any applicable restrictions.

Does the community have a pool?

Yes. Redwood Tree Apartments has a community swimming pool for residents. Pool access is subject to community rules and policies.

Is there a place to study on-site?

Yes. Residents have access to an on-site study room with comfortable seating, a complimentary self-serve coffee bar, and free printing.

Are furnished apartments available?

Furnished apartments may be available upon request for an additional monthly fee. Confirm current options and pricing with the leasing team.

Is laundry available?

The community offers on-site laundry facilities. Contact the leasing team for current facility details and access information.

How can I tour Redwood Tree Apartments?

Request a tour through the leasing team or schedule a self-guided tour at redwoodtreeapartments.mysmarttour.com.

How do I check availability or apply?

Use the “Check Availability” button to review current apartment availability, rates, deposits, and fees. Confirm all details before submitting an application.

Who can apply?

Redwood Tree Apartments welcomes applications from qualified applicants, including students and non-students. Applications are evaluated under the community's rental criteria and applicable fair housing laws.

What appliances are included in the apartments?

Redwood Tree Apartments offers fully equipped kitchens with major appliances, including a stove and refrigerator. Appliances and features may vary by apartment, so confirm the exact equipment included with an available home before applying.

Do apartments have balconies or patios?

Apartment homes may include a balcony or private patio. Outdoor-space features vary by floor plan and apartment location; ask the leasing team about current availability.

Is air conditioning available?

Yes. Air conditioning and heating are listed among the apartment amenities. Confirm the specific system and features included with the apartment you are considering.

Are apartments cable- and internet-ready?

Yes. Apartments are listed as cable- and internet-ready. Residents should contact service providers and the leasing team for current provider, installation, equipment, and billing information.

What lease terms are offered?

Redwood Tree Apartments advertises academic-year leases, summer leases, and flexible terms for students. Available lease lengths and dates may change, so contact leasing for the options offered for your desired move-in date.

What are the leasing office hours?

Published office hours are Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and Sunday closed. Holiday and seasonal hours may vary.

Are virtual tours available?

Yes. Virtual-tour options are available, including a self-guided tour and online floor-plan experiences. Contact leasing if you need help choosing the right touring option.

How close is Redwood Tree Apartments to UC Davis?

Redwood Tree Apartments is at 607 E 8th Street in Davis and advertises convenient access to UC Davis, including an approximately three-minute bike ride. Actual travel time depends on the campus destination, route, traffic, construction, and transportation method.

Who manages Redwood Tree Apartments?

Redwood Tree Apartments is managed by BLR Commercial Real Estate, Inc., a licensed California real estate broker, DRE License #01875689.

How can I contact management?

Use the website contact form or call 530-601-9631 to reach the Redwood Tree Apartments leasing and management team.

Is parking available?

Parking availability, assignment, permit requirements, guest parking, and any associated fees can change. Contact the leasing team for the parking options offered with a specific apartment.

Which utilities are included in rent?

Utility responsibility may vary by lease and apartment. Ask leasing for a current written list of utilities included in rent, utilities billed separately, billing methods, and services residents must establish directly.

What are the application fee, security deposit, and income requirements?

Application fees, deposits, screening criteria, income requirements, and required documentation may change. Review the current availability and application information, then contact leasing for the rental criteria that apply when you submit an application.

Are guarantors or co-signers accepted?

Guarantor and co-signer eligibility depends on the community's current rental criteria. Contact leasing before applying to confirm whether one is accepted and what documentation or qualification standards apply.

Is roommate matching available?

Roommate-matching availability is not guaranteed. Ask the leasing team whether any roommate resources are currently offered; applicants remain responsible for reviewing lease obligations and choosing compatible roommates.

What are the pet limits, restrictions, and fees?

Redwood Tree Apartments is pet-friendly, but animal limits, documentation, deposits, monthly charges, and restrictions may apply. Contact leasing for the current pet policy. Assistance animals are handled in accordance with applicable fair housing requirements.

Is smoking permitted?

Smoking rules may cover apartments, balconies, patios, and common areas. Request the current smoking policy from leasing before signing a lease.

How do residents request maintenance?

Residents should use the management team's current maintenance-request process or resident portal. Contact management for instructions and use the designated emergency procedure for urgent maintenance concerns.

Is renters insurance required?

Insurance requirements and coverage standards may change. Ask leasing whether renters insurance is required, the minimum coverage needed, and when proof of coverage must be provided.

Are short-term leases, lease takeovers, or subleases allowed?

Redwood advertises academic-year and summer lease options. Short-term leases, lease transfers, replacements, takeovers, and subleases are subject to current availability, lease terms, and written management approval. Contact leasing before making arrangements.

Is bicycle storage available?

Ask the leasing team about current bicycle parking or storage locations, access rules, and security recommendations. Residents should follow community rules and secure bicycles appropriately.

Are accessible apartments or accommodations available?

Prospective and current residents may contact management to ask about available apartment features or to request a reasonable accommodation or modification. Requests are considered in accordance with applicable fair housing laws.
