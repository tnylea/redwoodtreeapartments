Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Redwood Tree Apartments and its property manager, BLR Commercial Real Estate, Inc. (collectively, “Redwood Tree,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collect, use, disclose, and protect information in connection with redwoodtreeapartments.com and features that link to this Policy (the “Site”). Separate notices may apply to applications, screening, resident portals, payments, or other services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you contact us, request information, schedule a tour, submit a guest card, inquire about availability, or otherwise communicate, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, preferred contact method, desired move-in date, apartment preferences, message, and other information you provide.

If you proceed to an application or resident service, AppFolio or another authorized provider may request identity, address, income, employment, rental-history, screening, payment, or other application-related information under separate disclosures. Do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account information, government identification, or other sensitive information through a general contact message unless an authorized secure form specifically requests it.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Site, we and our providers may automatically receive Internet Protocol address, approximate location, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, links selected, visit dates and times, session activity, and cookie or similar identifiers.

Information from other sources

We may receive information from property-management platforms, listing services, referral sources, analytics providers, and other parties when permitted by law and combine it with Site information.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • respond to questions and communicate about availability, tours, applications, leasing, and resident services;
  • create and manage prospect or guest-card records and route inquiries to leasing personnel;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, analyze, and improve the Site and services;
  • understand website traffic, engagement, and outreach effectiveness;
  • prevent fraud, misuse, security incidents, and harmful activity;
  • comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, and agreements; and
  • carry out purposes disclosed at collection or with your direction or consent.

3. Contact Forms, Guest Cards, Jotform, and AppFolio

The Site uses a hosted Jotform contact form. Information submitted through that form may be processed by Jotform on our behalf and may be transmitted to or recorded in AppFolio’s property-management and guest-card systems so our leasing team can respond and manage inquiries. AppFolio also hosts availability listings, applications, resident-login features, and related services linked from or embedded in the Site.

These providers may collect technical information when content loads and process submitted information under their own terms and notices. Review the Jotform Privacy Policy and AppFolio Privacy Notice. Their notices govern independent activities; this Policy describes our use of information we receive.

4. Google Analytics and Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand Site use. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies, including the _ga cookie, to distinguish browsers and sessions and collect page visits, session statistics, approximate geolocation, and browser or device information. Google Analytics 4 uses Internet Protocol addresses at collection time to derive location information and then discards them before logging.

Learn more through Google Analytics data collection information and Google’s Privacy Policy. You can control cookies in browser settings or use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Blocking cookies may affect functionality.

You can use the “Cookie settings” link in the Site footer to accept, reject, or change optional categories. The Site stores your choice in your browser for up to 180 days, unless you clear browser storage sooner. Necessary local storage supports theme preferences and remembers your privacy choice. Browser “Do Not Track” signals are not interpreted consistently. We recognize an enabled Global Privacy Control signal and keep advertising and cross-site sharing technologies disabled for that browser.

5. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information:

  • to BLR Commercial Real Estate, Inc., property ownership, and personnel or contractors involved in leasing and property operations;
  • to providers supporting forms, guest cards, property management, hosting, analytics, communications, security, maintenance, and professional services, including Jotform, AppFolio, and Google;
  • to authorities, courts, regulators, or other parties when required or permitted by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, property, or service integrity;
  • in connection with a property, asset, management, financing, merger, or organizational transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards; and
  • with your direction, authorization, or consent.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If practices materially change, we will update this Policy and provide legally required choices.

6. Data Retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage prospect and leasing records, provide services, meet recordkeeping obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect security. Periods vary by information type, relationship, legal requirements, and operational need. Providers may retain information under their agreements and policies.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No website, transmission, or storage system is completely secure. Use authorized secure channels for sensitive application, screening, payment, or account information.
